## What it does

<!-- Stable description of the program. Carried forward from the previous note; edit only when the product itself changes.
     3 to 6 lines: what it is, who uses it, where it runs (URL/port/binary), how to start it. -->
Whole-brain connectome pipeline and embodied neural fly agents for adult
*Drosophila melanogaster*, built on FlyWire v783 (139,255
neurons, 10.5M synapses) with MuJoCo biomechanics (NeuroMechFly) and a GPU
perturbation kernel on AMD RX 7900 XTX.

Current connectome result: across 32 independent seed clusters (64 mirrored odor-steering layouts),
the real graph did not outperform degree-preserving shuffles (pooled p=0.7551); it outperformed
the in-degree-only random family (pooled p=0.001472). The real arm scored 56/64 and missed its
frozen gate.

Brain dynamics: with raw synapse-count weights, recurrent activity floods the mushroom body (79% of
Kenyon cells active, odour identity lost). Per-neuron normalised gain 35 restores a sparse, distinct
Kenyon-cell code, but mushroom-body output and descending neurons stay silent, so dopamine-gated
learning on that code is not yet demonstrated (`agent.verify_sparse_learning` records FAIL).
See the matched comparison (private note: CONNECTOME_BENEFIT) and the [capability map](docs/CAPABILITIES.md).

Correction 2026-10-02: p-values recomputed with seed clusters (mirrored layouts are not independent); old pooled values 0.6990 (shuffles) and 0.000489 (random) are now 0.7551 and 0.001472; conclusions unchanged: no measured advantage over degree-preserving shuffles, and the real graph remains ahead of the random-column family.

Key areas: five multi-omics data layers, embodied agent system
with checkpoint/restore, and a Rust/Mojo screening rebuild. The compound
screening engine and chemoinformatics database now live in the separate
`fly-pharma` repository. GPU physics on RX 7900 XTX is closed: correct physics was reached after three ROCm-fork fixes, but it did not beat six CPU workers, so CPU MuJoCo remains the backend; our own MuJoCo port is in progress (body engine (private note: BODY_ENGINE)). See GPU body port status (private note: GPU_BODY_PORT) and the
master plan (private note: FLY_WORLD_MASTER_PLAN) for the full program.

## Highlights

- Our own GPU body engine (MuJoCo 3.9.0 port in Mojo, any MJCF): the fly stands on its 44 servos with every body part on the floor, matching MuJoCo field by field, 22x MuJoCo's CPU throughput at 65,536 animals.
- Watch it live: engine and renderer in one process on the GPU, frames to mpv, inside a closed 40 mm cube.
- A second species: a generated jumping-spider body that stands in the same engine with no new engine code.
- One setting for every brain intervention: stimulate, inject, silence, cut, gain and slow neuromodulation by FlyWire cell selectors, changeable while the brain runs.
- The whole brain recorded: every neuron's spike count every window.

## Known limits

- The live view is slow motion: one 0.1 ms step costs ~24 ms on the floor, ~80 ms in the cube (64 animals); speedups (warm start, a wave per animal) not built.
- Contact scratch is ~197 KB per animal in the cube (fine at 1k animals, ~13 GB at 65k).
- Spider: contact force (qfrc_constraint) misses the 1e-5 bar by 1.5-5x while qacc passes; cause open. Anatomy approximate, no hydraulic legs.
- Only plane contacts: no stones, boxes or animal-on-animal contact; noslip not ported.
- The brain does not drive the body yet (gait generator and brain-in-the-loop are plan steps 3-5).
- Sugar still cannot teach PAM through modulation (53 OA -> PAM synapses); the explicit teacher stays.
- Old experiments not yet rerun through brain settings.
