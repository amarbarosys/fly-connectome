# Plasticity methods: 10 papers each

Pulled 2026-10-06 by `tools/paper_pull.py` (OpenAlex through the local crawl4ai server): per method a relevance search,
top 25 hits, the 10 most cited kept. Citation counts are OpenAlex's on that day. Context: every passing training method in
this repo trains a readout or a spider scaffold; learning inside the fly connectome fails because MBON/DN output is silent.

Caveats: automated ranking, not a curated review. Biology and computational-model papers are mixed; the four
structural/intrinsic/developmental queries were re-run with model terms after the first pull returned mostly wet-lab work.
Not returned and worth checking by name: Triesch's intrinsic plasticity rule, Izhikevich's dopamine-modulated STDP,
Lazar's SORN (STDP + intrinsic plasticity + synaptic normalisation in one network).
