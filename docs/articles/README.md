# The Fly: a six-article series

A whole fruit fly brain, a body, and a world, built and tested in four days. Written for general readers. Every number comes from the repository's docs, receipts and git log.

1. [The real wiring beat scrambled wiring at smell, and at nothing else yet](01-a-brain-in-a-computer.md): the connectome, running it on a home GPU, and the first honest test of whether wiring matters.
2. [The brain learned first; the fly improved only when its walker could use it](02-teaching-a-fly.md): the mushroom body, a flood and its fix, food and pain learning, and the credit bug.
3. [Sugar reaches the fly's mouth but never its teacher](03-senses-and-shortcuts.md): sense presets, the hunger sweep failure, and why taste cannot teach in this model.
4. [We rebuilt MuJoCo for the GPU, and trusted it only after planting bugs in it](04-building-a-body.md): our own physics engine, checked against MuJoCo one function at a time, and the float32 traps.
5. [The whole ground is a formula: an endless world in 64 bytes](05-a-world-to-live-in.md): terrain from a formula, our renderer, the fly and a jumping spider in one live world.
6. [Every result we trust first survived a check that could have failed](06-what-we-learned.md): the mistakes that taught the most, how we check ourselves, and what is still open.
